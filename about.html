Who we are

Welcome to the family.

A church community growing in faith, serving together, and blessing Kihiihi.

Our story

Kihiihi Community Church is a welcoming local church where people from every background can encounter God, find friendship, and discover a place to serve.

We believe church is more than a Sunday gathering. It is a community that prays together, shares life, cares for neighbours, and carries hope into the world.

Our values

Faith

We keep Jesus at the centre of everything we do.

Fellowship

We make space for honest relationships and belonging.

Community

We serve Kihiihi with generosity, compassion, and joy.
