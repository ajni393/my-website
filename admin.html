How to add social media videos
  1. Open the original video on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok and copy its public link.
  2. Choose Social video or Sermon, add a title, and paste the link into Video link.
  3. Choose whether to publish, then save. Published social videos appear on Watch & Connect; sermons appear in the sermon archive.

Instagram and Facebook content must be public and allow embedding. TikTok needs the full @username/video URL. Visitors choose “Load video” before a platform player loads; an original-platform link is always available if embedding is blocked. You do not upload video files or share your social media password.
